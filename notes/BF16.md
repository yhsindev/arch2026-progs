specified upper-half bits. Other inputs use nearest-even rounding by adding
0x7FFF plus the lowest retained bit.

Multiplication classifies the first operand before the second, preserving NaN

normalized before multiplying their eight-bit significands. The stored fraction
is truncated, including the contract's removal of the hidden bit before a

preserves the quiz's approximation rather than substituting a rounded root.
NaNs are preserved, negative nonzero values produce the canonical NaN, and
negative zero and positive denormals produce positive zero.
